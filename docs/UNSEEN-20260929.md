eight wrong; 82 questions abstain. Even directly relevant passages may omit
essential details or give outdated schedules. This is a deterministic source
audit, **not** six correct answers or a scored comparison with internet plus
a frontier model. The strict source gate is far below the required usefulness
standard. Do not submit this checkpoint as satisfying it.
